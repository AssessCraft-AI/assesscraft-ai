## Design Specificity Verdict
AssessCraft AI displays strong domain specificity for academic exam packaging (CLO alignment, Bloom levels, Version A/B parity, Board of Examiners sign-off). The new Admin panel brings clean token consistency. However, the core assessment workspace is an intimidating 3,425-line monolith that overwhelms users with dense metrics and lacks keyboard accessibility.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Missing Keyboard Accessibility & Power Navigation: No hotkeys for triaging items (J/K next, A approve, F flag). Forced mouse dependency slows power users. (/impeccable adapt, audit)
